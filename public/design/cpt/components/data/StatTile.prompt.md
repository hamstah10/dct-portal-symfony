Metric tile for power/torque figures and dyno readouts.

```jsx
<StatTile label="Leistung" value="285" unit="PS" delta="+38" gain />
<StatTile label="Drehmoment" value="450" unit="Nm" delta="+72" dark />
```

Mono tabular numerals. `gain` paints the value ignition-red; `delta` auto-colours by sign with a trend arrow; `dark` for a carbon tile.
